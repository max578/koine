# koine: Synthesised Fourth Opinion for Bayesian Triangulation

Synthesises one coherent, independent predictive opinion for a Bayesian
model from a curated battery of known and underutilised inference
methods, or abstains when no diversity gain exists. Built to serve as a
fourth, synthesised witness alongside an independent panel of inference
engines: it stacks over model-structure and method diversity in
predictive space (leave-one-out stacking, PSIS-gated) on an axis
orthogonal to the engine axis, never averaging the engines. Predictive
opinions are exchanged through an ArviZ-style 'DataTree' 'NetCDF'
interchange so no live 'Python' is required at read time. Abstention is
a first-class outcome.
